## Explicit exposed calculation regions

Reused installed Shapely 2.2.0 (BSD-3-Clause) `constrained_delaunay_triangles` to represent surfaces with physical exclusions as disjoint outer-loop polygons. Official API: https://shapely.readthedocs.io/en/stable/reference/shapely.constrained_delaunay_triangles.html . Unlike unconstrained triangulation with centroid filtering, this operation preserves polygon boundaries. The assignment adapter verifies both union symmetric difference and sum of triangle areas before writing a new experimental native copy. This establishes geometric equivalence only; actual DIALux calculation, exposed-surface readback and saved/reopened evidence remain separate acceptance gates. See `06_Tools/flatten_auxiliary_holes.py` and the per-region manifest.

DIAL's official guidance says automatic room main surfaces are appropriate for empty rooms and recommends manually modelling exposed surfaces when objects obscure room faces: https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000115449-room-main-surfaces . Physical column and luminaire openings are excluded from the auxiliary exposed ceiling; occupied task and surrounding regions are retained. The automatic and first custom ceiling results with near-zero minima remain recorded as failed checks, not relabelled as compliant results.
# Assignment PDF resource pruning

The complete 112-task native report shares large resource dictionaries. A 14-page PDFium-only excerpt exceeded 10 MB. Reused pikepdf 10.5.0 (MPL-2.0, installed locally from PyPI, no source vendored) and its documented `Pdf.remove_unreferenced_resources()` to copy explicit pages and remove unused resources. The bounded excerpt is 844,601 bytes. Independent PDFium verification preserved page text, physical dimensions and exact rendered pixels at both 72 and 150 dpi. See `06_Tools/compact_pdf_excerpt.py` and the excerpt manifest. This is lossless resource pruning, not resampling or recreating native results. Documentation: https://pikepdf.readthedocs.io/en/stable/api/main.html and https://pikepdf.readthedocs.io/en/stable/topics/pages.html .
